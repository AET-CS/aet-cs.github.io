# RPN Quiz Review - Answer Key

### 1. Evaluate

`6 2 / 3 + 4 *`

$6/2=3$, $3+3=6$, $6\times4=24$. **Answer: 24**

### 2. Convert to RPN

(7 - 2) × 3 + 8

**Answer:** `7 2 - 3 * 8 +`

### 3. Convert to algebraic notation

`a b - c d + *`

**Answer:** $(a - b)(c + d)$

### 4. Evaluate

`9 4 - 5 2 * +`

$9-4=5$, $5\times2=10$, $5+10=15$. **Answer: 15**

### 5. Convert to RPN

(p + q) × (r - s)

**Answer:** `p q + r s - *`

### 6. Convert to algebraic notation

`x y * z w - /`

**Answer:** $\dfrac{xy}{z - w}$

### 7. Evaluate

`30 sin 2 * 9 3 / 2 ^ -`

$\sin 30^\circ = 0.5$, $0.5\times2=1$, $9/3=3$, $3^2=9$, $1-9=-8$. **Answer: -8**

### 8. Convert to RPN

12 / (4 + 2) - 1

**Answer:** `12 4 2 + / 1 -`

### 9. Convert to algebraic notation

`15 3 / 4 +` (do not evaluate)

**Answer:** $\dfrac{15}{3} + 4$

### 10. Evaluate

`18 3 / 4 - 2 ^`

$18/3=6$, $6-4=2$, $2^2=4$. **Answer: 4**

### 11. Convert to RPN

$\sqrt{m^2 + n^2}$

**Answer:** `m 2 ^ n 2 ^ + sqrt`

### 12. Convert to algebraic notation

`p q r * + s t - /`

**Answer:** $\dfrac{p + qr}{s - t}$

### 13. Evaluate

`60 cos 4 * 25 sqrt +`

$\cos 60^\circ = 0.5$, $0.5\times4=2$, $\sqrt{25}=5$, $2+5=7$. **Answer: 7**

### 14. Convert to RPN

(9 + 3) / (2 × 2)

**Answer:** `9 3 + 2 2 * /`

### 15. Convert to algebraic notation

`a b c + * d -`

**Answer:** $a(b + c) - d$

### 16. Evaluate

`7 3 + 2 * 4 -`

$7+3=10$, $10\times2=20$, $20-4=16$. **Answer: 16**

### 17. Convert to RPN

(u - v)(w + z) / y

**Answer:** `u v - w z + * y /`

### 18. Evaluate

`8 3 2 * - 2 /`

$3\times2=6$, $8-6=2$, $2/2=1$. **Answer: 1**

### 19. Convert to RPN

$\dfrac{a+b}{c-d} \times e$

**Answer:** `a b + c d - / e *`

### 20. Convert to algebraic notation

`m n + p / q r * -`

**Answer:** $\dfrac{m+n}{p} - qr$
