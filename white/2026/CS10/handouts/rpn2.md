Using each number `2, 3, 4, 5` exactly once, and any of the operators `+ - * \ ^` any number of times:

<ol type="A">
<li>What expression yields the largest possible number?</li>
<li>What expression yields the smallest (most negative) number?</li>
<li>How close can you get to $e = 2.71828...$?</li>
<li>What is the smallest positive integer you <em>cannot</em> construct?</li>
</ol>

Examples (These are not answers!):

<ol type="A">
<li><code>2 3 4 5 * * * = 120</code></li>
<li><code>5 4 3 + - = -2</code></li>
<li><code>4 5 * 3 2 ^ / = 2.222</code></li>
<li><code>4 2 ^ 3 5 * - = 1</code></li>
</ol>
