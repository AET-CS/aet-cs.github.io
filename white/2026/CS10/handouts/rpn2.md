Using each number `2,3,4,5` exactly one, and any of the operators `+ - * \ ^` any number of times:

A. What expression yields the largest possible number
B. What expression yields the smallest (most negative) number
C. How close can you get to $e = 2.71828...$
D. What is the smallest positive integer you *cannot* construct.

Examples (These are not answers!):

A. `2 3 4 5 * * * = 120`
B. `5 4 3 + - = -2`
C. `4 5 * 3 2 ^ / = 2.222`
D. `4 2 ^ 3 5 * - = 1`
